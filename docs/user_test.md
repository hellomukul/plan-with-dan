# 15-minute user test (for the report's Impact section)

**Who:** one or two SOM classmates. A first-year planning Spring 2027 electives is ideal.
**What you need:** a stopwatch, the live app link, and this sheet.

## Part A: "the old way" (5 minutes max)

Ask: *"Using only the SOM course directory and your usual tools, find three Spring 2027 electives that fit your career goal and don't clash with each other. Tell me when you're done."*

- Time to finish: ______ min (stop at 5 if they're not done, and write "5+")
- How many courses did they shortlist? ______
- Did they check for clashes or half-term overlaps? Y / N
- Did they think about Global Studies or LDR? Y / N

## Part B: with Plan with Dan (5 minutes)

1. Create an account and do the 5-step quiz.
2. Click **Find my courses**, then **Run Dan's crew**.
3. Add the picks that fit. Open **My plan**.
4. Ask Dan one question of their own.

- Time from quiz finished to a clash-free plan: ______ min
- Number of picks they would actually bid on: ______ / ______
- Did any pick feel wrong or irrelevant? Which one, and why?
- Did they try **Calendar → Google / Apple** or the **Bid coach**?

## Part C: three questions (2 minutes)

1. On a scale of 1–10, how useful is this compared to how you plan today? ______
2. What would make you use it for real bidding?
3. One quote I can use in the report: "______________________________________"

## Record

- Screenshot their shortlist and plan. Get permission first, and blur names.
- Their quote, with first name or an initial only.
- Before and after times, filled into the report's Impact table.
