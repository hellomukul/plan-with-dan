The problem: planning an SOM semester is a part-time job

  • ~700 MBAs × 2 terms: ~100 electives, half-terms, a points auction, rules in 4 places
  • Clashes surface after add/drop; late changes cost $25
  • No public bid history: "enormous stress, anxiety, and frustration" (SOM student, 2018)
  • The hidden trap: 10 Global Studies days, and an IE needs all 10 unused

What's new

ExistingMissing for SOM students

SOM directory + auction appNo planning or clash help before bidding
ChatGPT Pulse · Gemini Daily BriefGeneric inbox briefs, no SOM rules or deadlines
ChatGPTNo SOM catalog: invents courses and times

Plan with Dan: an agent that can only recommend real courses and quotes the description to prove it. Clash checks on real dates, GSA-budget rules, an honest bid coach, and a calendar feed.

Safety: defenses that fired in testing

  • Injection blocked before the model: $0
  • Validator dropped invented / out-of-list picks
  • Caps: $0.05 per turn, $0.50 per day, loop guards
  • Fixed by testing: history poisoning, budget overrun, wrong EMBA section
  • Undo for every change · export / delete your data
  • 35 automated tests

Impact

  • Goal → clash-free shortlist in ~2 minutes instead of an evening
  • Every pick is a real course, with evidence and a backup
  • Saves rule mistakes (16-unit minimum, GSA budget, half-term clashes) before bidding
  • 1 hour saved × 700 students × 2 terms ≈ 1,400 hours / year for ≈ $3k / year
